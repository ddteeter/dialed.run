# Garmin (plan 152) · hold back from the public repo

Board: `Garmin Rulings.dc.html`. Frames marked GARMIN SUBMISSION are the mockups for Garmin's review and its 30-day notice of a new display of Garmin data. Nothing here is public until Garmin approves the app. The public Round 39 rulings (items 66–76) are in `docs/product.md`.

- **77 · Attribution.** In text, never the tag logo: "Garmin {device model}" (e.g. "Garmin Forerunner 970"), or "Garmin" alone when the model is unknown (the owner confirmed this). Phase 1 (the owner-only import) always shows "Garmin" because it can't learn the model; the full integration shows the model. `GarminAttribution`: text-small, weight 500, --ink-2 (#4E4E44 on paper, #DEDDD6 on ink), sentence case, never caps or mono. It's the first line under the view's title: A1's imported-run card, under D's headline, under E1's byline, once per row on R (a header line would claim the whole of a mixed list), and under the temperature on the share/OG entry card (26px at 1200×630), with the weather credit at the card's foot. runs.csv gets a `source` column per row ("Garmin {model}" / "Strava" / "File"). A run uploaded as a file draws nothing. The weather credit stays with the conditions, a step smaller, and never shares a line with Garmin's.
- **78 · Derived line.** "Derived in part from Garmin device-sourced data." Same step, directly under the figure's heading, shown only when at least one Garmin run is in that figure (server flag). E2 now; B1/B2 and M2–M4 later.
- **79 · Import from Garmin (owner only, phase 1).** A section of A1 under the file well: IMPORT FROM GARMIN · LAST 10. Row: date · distance · time, then "Garmin"; IMPORT › or [IMPORTED] › (opens the run). Loading: three rows with breathing brackets. None: "No runs on Garmin yet." Unreachable: §4a NOT LOADED · "Garmin didn't answer." · Try again. Already here: A1's duplicate receipt. Retired when push arrives.
- **80 · Connecting.** T1: Garmin's tile (their file, unaltered) + "Garmin Connect™" on one line, then the consent (approved), then our primary "Connect Garmin Connect™".
  - **What we receive.** Each run's time, duration, distance, start point rounded to about a kilometre, the device, and the effort you rated it on your watch. No heart rate, sleep or any other health data.
  - **How.** Garmin sends each new run once you connect. We read the file, keep those facts and delete the file.
  - **To stop.** Disconnect here or in Garmin Connect. Runs already here stay until you delete them.
  - **To delete.** Delete a run from the run itself, or your whole account under Settings › Account, or write to hello@dialed.run. We email you when it's done.
  - (desk@ is the appeals address and hello@ handles account requests. Settings › Your data holds only the export.)
  - Settings rows: not connected · "Your runs arrive on their own." · Connect; "Connected as {name}" · Disconnect; "Disconnected. Runs already here stay." · Connect; "Garmin Connect ended the connection. New runs won't arrive." · Connect again; full (approved): "Garmin connections are full for now. Strava still works." with no action.
  - Bell: "New run from Garmin: add your kit" → A2.
  - P3 offers both, Garmin Connect™ first ("Your runs arrive on their own."), then Strava ("So we can remind you. Optional."). The Garmin row isn't drawn while Garmin is full or unshipped. A Garmin import clears the matching Strava reminder silently.
- **81 · Effort from the watch.** The watch's 1–10 perceived-effort rating (never heart rate) prefills A3's effort chip: 1–4 Easy, 5–6 Steady, 7–8 Workout, 9–10 Race. It's drawn selected exactly like a runner's pick, and it's saved if left alone. The source is named beside EFFORT: FROM YOUR WATCH · RATED {n} OF 10 (MONO.xs, --muted); spoken "Effort, Steady, from your watch, rated 6 of 10". Any tap, even on the same chip, makes it the runner's pick, drops the source line and records effort_source = runner. It never re-prefills. No rating: nothing is picked and there's no source line.
