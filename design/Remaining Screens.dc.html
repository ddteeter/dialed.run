V1 SCOPE

Aggregate only — no individual cards, no follow buttons, no usernames. Nothing here identifies anyone, so it ships before any moderation work.

FEEDCLOSET+ ADDCALLYOU

Anonymous aggregate is the whole v1 social product — and arguably the more useful half. The verdict distribution replaces the stranger cards and answers the same question faster.

SORT FOR USEFULNESS

Following is chronological — social contracts demand it. Your conditions is ranked by conditions proximity, then verdict clarity. A "too cold" verdict is as valuable as a "dialed" one.

CONSENSUS BLOCK

Aggregate first, individuals second. Most runners want the answer, not the browsing — and the aggregate is defensible in a way a single stranger's photo isn't.

COLD-START PROOF

A new user with no follows lands on the conditions tab by default. It's the one social surface that works on day one — and in E2-lite form it needs no follow graph at all.

FINDING PEOPLE IN V1

The Following empty state points at username search, not the overlap-ranked Discovery screen — that ranking needs cohort math the first release won't have. You follow people you already know.

MODERATION NOTE

Outfit photos of real bodies need a reporting path and a "faces blurred by default" option before this tab opens to strangers. Design debt, flagged now.

THE HONESTY TEST

Gear gaps recommend categories, show the evidence that produced them, and include retirement suggestions. If affiliate revenue ever arrives, the "NOT PAID" chip flips to a disclosure rather than disappearing.

STILL NOT DESIGNED

Blocked-runners list, report flow, faces-blurred option, the desktop feed, and shoe mileage as its own object. Notifications, settings, Strava connect, manual entry, naming, and the Call teaser live in V1 Screens.

DECIDED

Laundry state lands with the call epic, not v1 — it matters most when a recommendation has to say "that's in the wash." Nothing in the market handles it, so it stays a differentiator worth building properly.
