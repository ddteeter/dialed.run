9 · When a control fails Confirmed, one addition: the failure band, sized to the thing that failed, directly under it. A form's band sits above its button because the button is the thing that failed. A control's band sits below the control because the control has to stay where the thumb left it. No pink line, no silent snap-back, anywhere.
9.1 · WHERE

Under the control's row, full content width. Never beside a pill — too narrow for a sentence and a button. Useful on D: under the Useful line. On the feed card: under Useful, inside the card. Follow on H: under the header's action row. A row control (Unblock, DS2, Strava): inside the row, below its content, so the band and the name it concerns stay one object. The control is back at its prior state while the band shows — the band says why.

9.2 · COPY

One rule, per-control words: the kicker names the state that is still true. That's what "Nothing saved", "Not signed in" and "Didn't load" already do. The sentence is the Form Contract's cause line, unchanged.

USEFUL  NOT MARKED · Your connection dropped.FOLLOW  NOT FOLLOWING · Your connection dropped.UNFOLLOW  STILL FOLLOWING · Your connection dropped.UNBLOCK  STILL BLOCKED · Your connection dropped.Our end: "Our end failed." in place of the connection line. A2 Attach: NOTHING ATTACHED. Strava: NOT CONNECTED / STILL CONNECTED.
9.3 · DISMISSAL

Stays until the next attempt, like a form's. Try again in the band and pressing the control itself are the same retry. Success removes the band; leaving the screen removes it. Never on a timer — a band that clears itself takes the retry with it, which is the toast problem. Appears and leaves in one frame. The status region announces the kicker and sentence; focus stays on the control, because the control is a retry.

9.4 · NOT OPTIMISTIC

Wait for the server; show the in-flight label. Optimistic-then-rollback is the snap-back this item exists to remove: the heart fills, the count ticks, then both reverse, and the runner has read a lie twice. [ Noting ], [ Following ], [ Unblocking ] per the Accessibility Contract, width fixed, aria-busy, re-entry guarded. The count changes on success only. An unblocked row leaves the list on success, not on press. [ Unblocking ] is new to the in-flight table.
