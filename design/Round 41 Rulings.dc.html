86a · PUBLIC comes back, meaning it The label is PUBLIC. The strings that still use "public" for signed-in sharing change, which round 27 already ordered. Round 27 retired the word because it was false. Now it's true, and any other word ("open", "anyone", "web") would be a euphemism for the one audience that most needs a plain name. The sub-line does the defining: anyone with the link, no sign-in.
