A refusal says when, not "try again".

One ruling covers every new refusal (324, 328, 60a, 329, 60d, 60e): a refusal a retry can't fix drops Try again and, where there is one, says when it can work. A field error only where typing in the field fixes it. Drawn: the link landings' ask state and the runs row with a badge. A manual run's title stays the runner's own.

The refusal rule
Fixable in the field → the field error, under the field (R29 #1). The runner's next keystroke is the fix. 329 #3, 60c.
Fixable by retrying → the §4a band under the control, kicker naming the state still true, with Try again.
Not fixable now (limits, holds, refusals, gone) → the same band and slot, no Try again. A limit names its {time} or {date}; a refusal names the other way, if there is one.
