♥ 24 USEFUL

No comments in v1 — "useful" is the only reaction. Discussion, replies, and the saved-kit count arrive with the social milestone, and each one needs a moderation and reporting path before it ships.

"USEFUL" INSTEAD OF "LIKE"

The feed is reference, not performance. Renaming the primary reaction changes what people post — and gives the recommender a far better signal than a like. This is locked, not a variant.

THE VERDICT IS PUBLIC HERE

A shared run carries its verdict badge — that's what makes the post worth reading. Said plainly at the share toggle (A3) and in Settings, because a surprise here is a trust problem.

DEFERRED, NOT CUT

"Try this kit" is the bridge from social to utility — it maps someone else's kit onto what you own and flags what you're missing. It returns with saved kits, and it's where gear suggestions eventually earn their place.

GEAR ATTRIBUTION

Brand names appear on kit rows but never as logos or links in v1. Keeps the surface honest before any commercial relationship exists.

INTAKE REALITY

GPX is the only path that needs no partnership, so it's designed as the happy path — not a fallback. Apple Health is framed as "on the roadmap" rather than promised.

LARGE WARDROBES

No body-part wizard — six sequential screens would break the 15-second target for the 90% case. Instead the run's own conditions filter the closet by default, category rows show match counts, and a sheet (A2b) carries search and manual filters for the rest. The picker gets better as a closet grows, because more history means a tighter filter.

RESPONSIVE STRATEGY

Logging and the call are mobile-first (they happen at the door). The closet is desktop-first (it happens on the couch). Both work everywhere; neither is designed for the other's context first.

NOT YET DESIGNED

Onboarding and first-wardrobe setup, add-a-garment, the feed itself, profiles, follow and discovery. Onboarding is the next real risk: nobody photographs 40 garments on day one.

OPEN DECISIONS

Three-state or five-state verdicts. How much social signal enters the call. Whether verdicts are ever public. All three are togglable or noted rather than assumed.
