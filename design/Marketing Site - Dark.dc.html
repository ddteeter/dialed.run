How it works

Every run is a measurement: what you wore, what the weather was, and how it felt. dialed.run keeps a record per garment and per temperature range, so the advice is based on your clothes and how you run warm or cold, not on a chart.

The verdict

WAY COLDA BIT COLDDIALEDA BIT WARMWAY WARM

One tap after the run, plus which piece was off if one was.

Questions

Do I need Strava?No. Strava saves you a step. You can also upload a GPX or FIT file from any watch.
Where does the weather come from?Visual Crossing, for the time and place your run started. You never type it.
Who sees my runs?Only you, unless you share it with runners on dialed.run. Either way, its kit and verdict can count in the guides, added up with other runners' and never with your name.
Can I keep my runs out of the guides?Yes. In Settings, turn off Count my runs in anonymous totals. Your runs leave the guides at the next nightly update.
Why invite-only?So we can answer everyone who writes in. Request an invite and we'll send a code.

"The loop" and "The call" sections follow M1's steps at lead length. They're not drawn because they're prose only. Owner to confirm "Why invite-only?". Structured data: FAQPage.

METHOD

Shared and private runs from confirmed accounts, minus runners who opted out. Only the band, sky, kit and verdict are counted, never notes, photos, times or places. A figure needs 20 or more runners for a brand and 5 or more for a band. Pieces are counted by catalogue model, and unmatched pieces are left out. Region only counts runners who opted in. dialed.run is invite-only, so these are early, enthusiastic runners, not all runners.
