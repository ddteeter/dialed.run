R22 · 3 ›
SEPARATION

Posts are divided by a 10px gutter of darker ground (#E3E2D8 light, #1E1E24 dark), never a line. At desk the page stays chalk and the band runs the full window width; content stays at 620. No fades, no radius, no column rules. Hairlines belong to the run strip alone.

MATCH FLOOR

Five runners or nothing. Under five in 3 days, widen to 14 once, and say so. The band never widens.

R22 · 4 ›
WHO SHOWS

Only SHARED entries. Unverified runners save PRIVATE with no queue, so nothing lands later. Deleted entries leave the feed at once.
