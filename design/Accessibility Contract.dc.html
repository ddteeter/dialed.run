Readable in the dark, at a run, by anyone.

A runner opens this at 5:40 AM with one glove on, or reads it aloud through a screen reader, or navigates it from a keyboard because that's how they navigate everything. The rules below are already half-stated across five files. This is the one place they all are, plus what each lane has to test before a PR is done.

X1 · Ten rules Each one is already a decision somewhere in the system. Collected so no lane has to find it.
01 · NEVER HUE ALONE

Verdicts carry the three-slot position mark and a word. Errors carry border weight and a hi-viz band. Coverage is ink density. Pink, teal, grey and hi-viz are accelerators — remove every colour and the meaning survives. (AB, Form Contract §2.)

02 · CONTRAST FLOORS

Body text 4.5:1. Headline scale (≥24px Archivo Black) 3:1. Text on any accent surface is ink #0B0B0E. #9A9A90 and #7A7A70 on paper are decoration-only — never the sole label of a control; that is --label #6E6E64 (4.6:1). Teal as text is --dialed-text #00776A on paper (4.9:1); #009F8C is retired. Opacity never encodes meaning.

03 · 44PX TARGETS

Every tappable thing has a 44×44 hit area, including MONO.xs chips (10px, the type floor — nothing is drawn smaller) — pad the target, not the glyph. Adjacent targets have 8px between hit areas. Tab bar items are 44 tall regardless of the drawn height. Standalone targets only: a link set in a sentence takes WCAG 2.5.8's inline exception and does not grow the line.

04 · ONE H1 PER SCREEN

Every screen has exactly one <h1>: the Archivo Black headline. Mono captions are <h2> when they head a group, otherwise plain text. Onboarding cards carry their own h1 (AE). A screen with no heading is a bug.

05 · NATIVE ROLES

Toggles are role="switch". Chip groups are role="group" with a label; each chip is a <button aria-pressed>. Segmented controls are role="radiogroup". Sheets are role="dialog" aria-modal and trap focus. Nothing interactive is a <div onClick>.

06 · FOCUS IS VISIBLE AND SQUARE

2px solid outline, offset 2px, in the text colour of the ground (ink on paper, paper on ink). Never removed, never replaced by a colour change alone. Pill buttons get a pill outline; everything else gets the brand's square corners. Focus order follows visual order, top to bottom, left to right.

07 · NEVER DISABLED

aria-disabled="true" plus a handler guard. True disabled drops the element from the tab order and hides why. (Form Contract §5.) The one exception is a control that is genuinely absent from the product state, which should not render at all. Two unavailable states, two costumes. In flight: the label swaps to breathing brackets (Form Contract §5) with aria-busy; no dimming. Not yet: the control is drawn at full strength and stays silent; the thing it needs is stated once, in the screen's own copy, not on the button.

08 · ANNOUNCE ONCE, POLITELY

One role="status" region per screen. Failures, "Copied", "Saved", and W3's three face-sentences write to it. Success is silent unless the runner did something (AF). Never aria-live="assertive" — nothing in this product is an emergency.

09 · MOTION HAS A FLOOR, NOT A ZERO

prefers-reduced-motion collapses every transition to a 90ms opacity change. Nothing loops except the breathing brackets, which hold still at full opacity under reduced motion. No parallax, no autoplay. (Motion Doctrine NEVER list.)

10 · EVERY GRAPHIC HAS WORDS

Coverage bars, verdict marks, day strips, the three-slot mark, weighting bars — each is role="img" with an aria-label that says the whole thing in a sentence. Decorative placeholders and icons are aria-hidden. Icon-only buttons have a name.

X3 · What to test Per PR, per screen. Automated where it can be; the rest is a five-minute manual pass a lane does before asking for review.
AUTOMATED · CI FAILS THE PR
01axe-core on every route in both themes. Zero violations at AA. Contrast checks run against computed colour, not the token name.
02Exactly one h1 per route. No heading level skipped.
03No disabled attribute on any button or input. Lint rule.
04No onClick on a non-interactive element. Lint rule.
05Every interactive element's hit area ≥ 44×44 — measured in a Playwright pass, not asserted from CSS.
06Every role="img" has a non-empty label. Every <svg> has aria-hidden or a title.
07Snapshot under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — no element has an animation longer than 90ms.
KEYBOARD · FIVE MINUTES, EVERY SCREEN
01Tab from the top. Every interactive element is reached, in visual order, and the focus ring is visible on every one — including on hi-viz and pink grounds.
02Nothing is reachable that isn't visible. Nothing visible is unreachable. Decorative placeholders are skipped.
03Open every sheet and dialog. Focus moves in. Tab cycles inside. Escape closes. Focus returns to what opened it.
04Radiogroups and segmented controls: arrow keys move, space selects, tab leaves the group in one press.
05Submit a form with two errors. Focus lands on the summary. Each focus button in the summary lands on its field.
06An aria-disabled button is still tabbable and pressing it does nothing visible except the failure band, if any.
SCREEN READER · VOICEOVER + TALKBACK, PER EPIC
01Log a run end to end with the screen off. The verdict announces as a radio; the "noted" block announces once via the status region.
02Read the Call. The forecast, the kit, the confidence and the coverage bar each come through as one sentence, not a list of hex-coloured divs.
03Tap through O3 and hear the counter change without leaving the chip.
04Upload a photo on the web. Hear "Checking this photo", then either "We blurred one face" or "We couldn't look for faces" — never silence, never twice.
05Copy a share link. Hear "Link copied" once. Copy again within two seconds — hear it once more, not stacked.
06Open the banned notice. The heading reads first; the reason reads as a quotation; the email is the only link.
ALSO CHANGED THIS ROUND

Tab-bar inactive labels moved from #9A9A90 to #6E6E64 across all light artboards — 2.8:1 was fine for a glyph, not for the word beside it. Dark files inherit on the next pass.

NOT DECIDED HERE

Dynamic type. The artboards are drawn at one size; the build should respect the OS text size to at least 200% without clipping, and that needs its own pass once the components exist. Flag it, don't guess it.

THE STANDARD

WCAG 2.2 AA. Not because a checklist says so — because the whole product is "read this in bad light with cold hands and trust it." That is an accessibility brief before it's anything else.
