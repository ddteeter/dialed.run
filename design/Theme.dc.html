Theme  /  light + dark  /  token pairs dialed.run

Two grounds, one meaning.

The app follows the system theme by default; a runner can pin either from the You tab. Every colour in the product is a role with a light value and a dark value. Roles that carry meaning — verdict hue, hi-viz failure, pink action — keep their meaning on both grounds, and text on an accent surface is always ink. The theme changes where you read from, never what you read.

01 · HUE MEANS THE SAME THING

Pink is cold and action, teal is dialed, grey is warm, hi-viz is failure — on both grounds. Teal and pink-as-text shift one step for contrast (AB already specified the grey). The verdict mark's slot position does the work either way.

02 · TEXT ON AN ACCENT IS INK

Pink, teal and hi-viz surfaces carry #0B0B0E text in both modes. Paper on pink is [3.0:1]; ink on pink is [7.1:1]. The accents are the one place the ground doesn't reach.

03 · COVERAGE INVERTS

Solid / hatch / hairline is drawn in the text colour, so it flips with the ground and stays monochrome. Ink is knowledge on paper; paper is knowledge on ink.

04 · HEADERS INVERT TOO

The ink header block on a paper screen becomes a paper header block on an ink screen. That's the brand's contrast move, not a bug — the status bar and headline sit on the bright block in dark mode. If a lane finds it too loud on a specific screen, the fallback is panel (#16161B) with a hairline, never a third grey.

T3 · The switch Follows the system by default. The override lives in the You tab header, next to the bell — three-way, because "back to following the system" has to be a choice you can make, not a state you fall into.

SWITCHING IS A CUT

No cross-fade. The doctrine has no surface for a whole-screen colour transition, and a 300ms fade between grounds is exactly the "ambient motion" the NEVER list bans. The ground changes on the frame after the tap.

THE SEGMENT SAYS WHAT IT'S DOING

The line under the header states the current source — "following your phone" or "pinned to dark" — so a runner whose app looks different from their phone knows why, and how to undo it.

FOR THE LANES

One data-theme attribute on the root, values light | dark, resolved from prefers-color-scheme unless a stored override exists. Every colour in the app comes from a variable in T1; a raw hex in component code is a review failure. Web uses color-scheme so native controls and scrollbars follow. Photos and their placeholders are unaffected except the placeholder fill.

THE DESK IS ALWAYS DARK

It's a tool, not the product, and it doesn't read the runner's theme. What marks it as operator-only is its chrome — the DESK label, the left rail, hi-viz as the only accent — not its ground. D0 is updated to say so.
