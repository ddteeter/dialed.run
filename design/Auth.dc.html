Auth.

Round 22, items 1 and 2. Two forms, four failures, one arrival. Every state is the Form Contract applied verbatim; the only new thing is where the page sits. Rest of round 22 is on Round 22 Coverage.

The rulings
1 · WRONG PASSWORD IS A FIELD FAILURE

The fix is inside the form, so it's yellow, on Password. "That password doesn't match this email." One message, never "email or password is wrong" split across two fields. A server fault is the band above the button: "Our end failed. Try again in a moment."

1 · THE BAND'S TWO WORDS

"Not signed in", not "Nothing saved". Auth saves nothing, so the contract's opener would be false. Same block, same place, same Try again. This is the only form that changes the two words.

1 · GOOGLE IS A SUBMIT BUTTON

In flight: brackets breathe, label keeps going. "[ Opening Google ]", hairline pill, width fixed. Its error is a failure band above the Google button, not above Log in; the pink is removed. Cancelled in the popup is not an error: return to rest, silently.

1 · SIGNED OUT ARRIVAL

A square notice above the heading, email prefilled, focus on Password. It names what's kept, not why the session died. After Log in, the runner lands on the filled form they left, and nothing re-submits on its own.

2 · THE SHELL

Auth takes the landing bar with no action. From 720 up: wordmark only, links to /. The panel drops its own wordmark, so there is one. The form's cross-link does the bar's job; "Log in" on the log-in page would point at itself. Below 720: no bar, the page's wordmark does it.

2 · THE TAB BAR, SIGNED OUT

Never. Every tab is a signed-in place; showing them to someone signed out is five links to the log-in page. Any signed-out page (auth, 404, a shared entry) gets no tab bar and no product bar. Desktop Contract DS3 "Nothing to draw" row now points here.

COPY

"Log in" and "Create account", matching the landing bar. Never "Sign in", "Sign up" or "Register" on screen; routes can keep their names.
