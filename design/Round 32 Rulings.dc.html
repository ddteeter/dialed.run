Round 32  /  anonymous totals · what feeds the guides and reportsdialed.run

Private means nobody sees it. It doesn't have to mean it never counts.

Ruling: the middle path, with five conditions. Copy changes are already applied to both Marketing boards and recorded in docs/product.md. The Terms and Privacy wording at the foot is for the owner's legal review and isn't final.

01 · The honest read
WHY YES

The bias argument decides it, more than volume does. A guide built only from shared runs is a guide built from the runs people were proud of, so it would overstate "dialed" exactly where runners need the truth. Private verdicts are the most candid data the product has. Deciding now costs nothing. Deciding after launch costs a Terms version and an acceptance prompt for everyone.

HOW RUNNERS WILL TAKE IT

Most won't mind, if they're told plainly and once, at the moment they choose. The runners who will mind are the ones for whom privacy means safety. Their fear is where and when: a route, a start time, a house. That's why the Strava heatmap comparison doesn't hold. A heatmap is made of locations, and these totals have none. So every disclosure has to lead with what isn't counted. "Never who, where or when" is the line that earns trust.

WHAT WOULD BREAK IT

Two things: a runner finding out from a screenshot instead of from us, and a figure small enough to point at one person. Those are conditions A and B below. If either one can't be met at launch, ship shared-only and revisit. Don't ship the middle path half-done.

A · Counted fields onlyTotals read the feels-like band, sky, month, garment type and catalogue model, and the verdict. Region is read only if the runner set it. Never the note, photo, route, start time, start place, handle or any free text. This is the claim every disclosure makes, so the aggregate job must not have read access to the rest.
B · Thresholds per figureEvery number on the page needs 5 or more distinct runners (20 for a brand), not just the page as a whole. That covers every row, every sky section, every split sentence and every region slice. A cell below the line is left out, never shown as "fewer than 5". Region slices also need 5 or more in the region.
C · Who countsConfirmed-email accounts only, so a burst of throwaway accounts can't move a guide. Also excluded: removed or quarantined entries, banned accounts, accounts pending deletion, and manual-temperature runs (existing rule).
D · What opting out undoesGuides are rebuilt nightly, so an opted-out runner is gone by morning. Reports are published once and stay as they are. The setting says both, in plain words. Opting back in takes effect from the next build. Deleting an account is an opt-out.
E · Told at the moment of choiceThe runner is told in three places: under the PRIVATE state of the share switch, in Settings, and in Au2's legal line. Each one links to the setting. In-app social proof ("worn by N runners") stays shared-only, as proposed. Closet pieces count only when worn on a counted run, as proposed.

04 · Terms and Privacy · for legal reviewThese are drafts in the product's voice, not legal text. Both go into the first published version of each document.
TERMS · ANONYMOUS TOTALS

When you log a run, you let us combine its feels-like temperature, sky, month, the kit you wore and your verdict with other runners' runs, and publish the totals. This covers runs you share and runs you keep private. We use these totals for the What to wear guides, seasonal reports and similar pages, and we may publish the totals as data that others can reuse. A total never includes your name, handle, notes, photos, route, or when or where you started. We only publish a figure when at least 5 runners are behind it, or 20 if it's about a brand. You can stop your runs counting at any time in Settings. That takes effect for anything we build after you change it. Totals we've already published stay as they are.

PRIVACY POLICY · ANONYMOUS TOTALS

We add up some details of every run (feels-like temperature band, sky, month, kit and verdict) across many runners to make public guides and reports. Private runs are included: "private" means no other person can see the run, not that it's never counted. We never read your notes, photos, route, start time or start place for this. No figure is published unless at least 5 runners are behind it, or 20 for a brand, so a total can't point to you. If you've chosen to add your region, we use it only to group totals, such as "runners in Minnesota", and only when at least 5 runners share that region. Your region is never shown on your profile or your runs. To opt out, turn off "Count my runs in anonymous totals" in Settings. Your runs are removed from the guides at the next nightly update and left out of future reports. Deleting your account opts you out too.
