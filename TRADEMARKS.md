## What you may do without asking

- **Say what your project is based on.** "A fork of dialed.run", "built on
  dialed.run's code", "compatible with dialed.run": in plain text, accurate,
  and no more prominent than your own project's name.
- **Write about dialed.run**, in reviews, articles, talks and posts, and link
  to it.
- **Run the code privately**, for yourself or for testing, under any name.

## What needs our permission

- **Running a copy for other people under the name dialed.run**, or a name
  close enough to be mistaken for it: a hosted service, an app in a store, a
  domain name or a social account.
- **Using the logo, wordmark or app icons** anywhere outside an unmodified
  copy of this repository.
- **Suggesting that we endorse or run your project** when we don't.

## If you run a fork for other people

The AGPL lets you, and it requires you to offer your users your modified
source. Before you do, give it a name of its own: replace the name in its
copy and page titles, and replace the logo, wordmark and app icons. The
code, the layouts and the features are yours to keep under the AGPL; the
name and the brand files are not.
