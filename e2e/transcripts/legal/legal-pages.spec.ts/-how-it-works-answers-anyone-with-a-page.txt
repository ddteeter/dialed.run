region, How the guides are made
heading, How the guides are made, level 2
paragraph
We will publish guides to what runners wear at each temperature. They count private runs too, without names. That keeps them honest: shared runs alone would make it look as if runners get it right more often than they do.
end of paragraph
term
Counted
end of term
definition
the temperature band, the sky, the month, the broad climate zone, the gear worn and how it felt.
end of definition
term
Never counted
end of term
definition
your name, notes or photos, when your run started, or where it was beyond the climate zone.
end of definition
term
The floor
end of term
definition
a figure needs at least # runners, or # if it names a brand. Counts are rounded to the nearest #, and only confirmed accounts count.
end of definition
term
Turning it off
end of term
definition
Settings › Privacy › "Count my runs in anonymous totals". Your runs leave the guides at the next rebuild, within a month.
end of definition
end of region, How the guides are made
region, Questions
heading, Questions, level 2
heading, Who sees my runs?, level 3
paragraph
A private run is seen by you alone. A shared run is seen by runners signed in to
dialed.run
. A kit you make public can be seen by anyone, even people without an account, and it never shows when or where you ran. Where you ran is never shown to anyone.
end of paragraph
heading, Does it connect to Strava?, level 3
paragraph
Only to remind you to log your kit after a run.
link, Strava's API agreement
limits what apps like ours may keep, so we never store the activity: not your route, your pace or anything else about it. Disconnect in Settings.
end of paragraph
heading, And Garmin?, level 3
paragraph
Not yet. Connecting Garmin is coming. Until then, export the run from Garmin Connect and upload the file.
end of paragraph
heading, How do I delete my account?, level 3
paragraph
Settings › Account › Delete account. You have # days to change your mind, then it is gone, and from our backups within # days. You can export your data first, from the same screen. The
link, privacy policy
has the details.
end of paragraph
