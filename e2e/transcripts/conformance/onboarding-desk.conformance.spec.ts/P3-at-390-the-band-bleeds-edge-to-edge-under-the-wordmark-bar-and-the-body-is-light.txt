paragraph
What we collect:
your Strava athlete id, a token we use only to disconnect, and, for # days, the id, type and time of each new activity so we remind you once.
How:
through Strava's sign-in and its activity notifications.
Never:
your activity details, route, pace or anything else about the run.
end of paragraph
paragraph
To stop:
Disconnect in Settings, or remove dialed.run under Strava › Settings › My Apps. Either deletes what we hold from Strava, apart from what we need to finish disconnecting, and we email you to confirm it.
