## How this record is built

**Derive, don't mirror.** The privacy policy is the source for what data
is held, why, who receives it and how long it is kept, and
`privacy-policy-sources.md` maps each of those claims to code. This record
does not restate them. Each row below names:

- the **policy section** that lists the data (a heading in
  `privacy-policy.md`);
- the **legal-bases row** that gives its basis (the table under "Why we
  use your data (legal bases)");
- the **retention row** (the table under "How long we keep it");
- the **processor rows** (the table under "Who else handles your data").

Where the policy says nothing, this record says so, gives a proposed
answer marked `[CHECK: …]`, and lists it under
[Gaps in the policy](#gaps-in-the-policy). A change to the policy is a
change to this record; check it in the same PR.

## Security measures (Art. 30(1)(g), Art. 32)

The policy's "Security" section, and its sources row, are the general
description: HTTPS with HSTS, hashed passwords, encryption at rest by
Cloudflare, owner-only garment photos, private kits returning nothing to
others, operator access only for named purposes (D-121). This record adds
what the policy does not say:

- Admin access is a list of ids in a secret (`ADMIN_USER_IDS`;
  `safety/admin.ts` `requireAdmin`); today one person.
- Each Workers binding is reached only through `src/env` (CLAUDE.md).
- Secrets are Workers secrets, never in the repository
  (`docs/deployment.md` §5).
- D1 has 30 days of point-in-time restore; R2 has no backup
  (`docs/deployment.md` §9–§10).
- Evidence outside the app (NCMEC report context, takedown notes, the
  breach register) is in a private R2 bucket with no Worker binding,
  reached by an owner-only token scoped to it, and locked against deletion
  (D-148). Its objects are deleted by hand when the record they back is
  swept; the breach register is kept indefinitely.
- Session tokens and Strava refresh tokens are stored in plain text
  (policy "Security").
- What to do when something goes wrong: the
  [breach response runbook](breach-response-runbook.md).
