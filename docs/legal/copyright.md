# [dialed.run] copyright and DMCA notice

**Version 1.** Effective: [OWNER: the day the first invite is sent]

Runners post photos, product names and links on [dialed.run]. If you think
something here uses your work without permission, this page says how to tell
us and what happens next. It follows the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act
(DMCA), 17 U.S.C. § 512. You can use it wherever you are.

For anything that is not about intellectual property, report it in the app
or write to desk@dialed.run (see the [terms](/terms)).

## What a notice must contain

A notice only counts if it has all six of these, in writing:

1. **Your signature**, physical or electronic (typing your full name is
   enough), as the copyright owner or someone authorised to act for them.
2. **The work** you say is infringed. If there are several, a list of them.
3. **The material** you say infringes, and enough to find it: a link to the
   kit or photo, or the runner's username and the date of the kit.
4. **How to reach you**: your name, postal address, phone number and email
   address.
5. **This statement:** "I have a good faith belief that use of the material
   in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its
   agent, or the law."
6. **This statement:** "The information in this notification is accurate,
   and under penalty of perjury, I am the owner, or authorized to act on
   behalf of the owner, of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed."

Before you send one, think about whether the use might be fair use or
otherwise allowed. Under 17 U.S.C. § 512(f), anyone who knowingly
misrepresents that material infringes can be liable for the damages that
causes.

## What happens next

- **We take the material down.** When we receive a notice that has all six
  elements, we remove the kit or photo it names. Removal deletes it from
  [dialed.run]; we keep a record of what was removed, when and why.
- **We tell the runner who posted it**, in the app and by email, that it was
  removed because it uses someone else's work. We may send them a copy of
  your notice, including your name and contact details, so that they can
  respond.
- If a notice is missing elements but tells us how to reach you, we will
  write back to ask for them.

## Counter-notice

If your kit or photo was removed because of a copyright notice and you
believe that was a mistake, or that the material was misidentified, you can
send a counter-notice to the same agent. It must contain, in writing:

1. **Your signature**, physical or electronic.
2. **What was removed**, and where it appeared before it was removed (a
   link, or your username and the date of the kit).
3. **This statement:** "I swear, under penalty of perjury, that I have a
   good faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result
   of mistake or misidentification of the material to be removed or
   disabled."
4. **Your name, postal address and phone number**, and a statement that you
   consent to the jurisdiction of the US Federal District Court for the
   judicial district where your address is (or, if your address is outside
   the United States, any judicial district in which [dialed.run] may be
   found), and that you will accept service of process from the person who
   sent the original notice, or their agent.

When we receive a valid counter-notice, we send a copy to the person who
sent the original notice. We keep removed material hidden for 30 days. If
a valid counter-notice reaches us in that time, we put it back 10 to 14
business days later unless the sender tells us they have gone to court.
After 30 days with no counter-notice, it is deleted. If, 10 business days after we sent the copy,
they have not told us that they have filed a court action to stop you, we
tell you, and you are free to post the material again. A notice answered
this way does not count against your account.

## Repeat infringers

We close the accounts of runners who repeatedly infringe copyright.

- **Every takedown is recorded** against the account that posted the
  material.
- **An account that has had material removed for infringement three times**
  is closed. A notice that was withdrawn, or that a counter-notice answered
  without a court action following, does not count.
- **We may close an account sooner** where the infringement is plain and
  deliberate.
- **Closing works as any other account closure** under the [terms](/terms):
  the runner can no longer sign in, their kits, photos and profile are
  hidden from everyone else, and we email them the reason. They can appeal
  to desk@dialed.run, with no deadline.

## Trademarks and other rights

For a complaint about a trademark or any other right that is not copyright,
such as a misleading product name, write to copyright@dialed.run with what
the material is, where it is, and what right you say it breaks. These are not
DMCA notices, and we handle them under the [terms](/terms).
