## Who sees what

- **Kits are shared by default.** You choose shared or private for each kit
  when you give the verdict, and you can make private your default in
  settings. Private kits never appear in feeds or in anyone's conditions
  numbers. They do count, without your name, in
  [anonymous totals](#your-content), unless you turn that off.
- **"Shared" means other signed-in runners on [dialed.run]**, not the open
  web. A shared kit, a profile and their photos are shown only to someone
  signed in. A link to a kit shared elsewhere previews as a generic
  [dialed.run] card, with nothing from the kit on it.
- **Some things are never shown to other runners**, such as your closet as
  a whole, your garment photos, notes and flags on garments, and your run's
  location. The privacy policy has the full list, and says when the people
  who run [dialed.run] may see them.
- **Garments you have worn are retired, not deleted.** When you remove a
  garment that is in one of your kits, we retire it so your old kits still
  make sense. You can choose instead to delete it and take it out of those
  kits.

## Screening, reports and moderation

- **Automated screening.** Every photo is checked by an automated moderation
  service (OpenAI's) before anyone else can see it. A photo it scores high
  on sexual, violent or self-harm content is held back from everyone but you
  until a person looks at it. New usernames are screened too (see
  [Your username](#your-username)). Automated screening only holds things
  back; it never removes content or closes accounts.
- **Reports.** Any signed-in runner with a confirmed email can report a kit,
  a photo, a profile or a product name. A kit you report leaves your feed
  straight away, and a runner you report leaves your search. When three different runners report the same kit or photo,
  it is hidden from everyone but its owner until a person reviews it.
- **Intimate images shared without consent** are the exception. One report
  that a kit or photo is an intimate image shared without consent hides it
  from everyone but its owner at once, and a person reviews it within 48
  hours. If it is what the report says, we remove it. Reporting something
  as this when you know it is not breaks these rules.
- **A person decides.** Removing content and closing accounts are decisions
  a person makes, not the automated screening. We do not watch everything
  posted and are not obliged to, but we act on what we find or are told.
- **What we can do** when something breaks these terms or the law: remove
  it, change a username, or close the account.
- **We tell you why.** When we remove your kit or photo, we tell you in the
  app and by email what was removed and which rule it broke. When we close
  your account, we email you the reason, and the app shows it when you try to
  sign in. The one exception is suspected child sexual abuse material, below.
- **A closed account** cannot sign in, and its kits, photos and profile are
  hidden from everyone else. We may close an account without warning when
  the breach is serious, or when the law or someone's safety requires it.

### Appeals

If you think we got a moderation decision wrong, write to desk@dialed.run,
or reply to the email that told you about it. **There is no deadline.** A
person reads every appeal. If we were
wrong, we undo what we can: a reopened account's kits come back as they
were. A kit or photo we removed has been deleted and cannot be brought back,
so we will tell you, and you are free to post it again.

If you are in the European Union, you may also be able to take a moderation
decision to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body, or to a court.
