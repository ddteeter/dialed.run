Ready for all-day-adventures or repeat sessions, the Repeat Merino Tee is made from our naturally thermoregulating, exceptionally odor-resistant merino blend that's as soft as it is strong, designed to be fresh and comfy when you reach for it, again and again.

Features

A Higher Standard
Responsible Wool Standard certified merino ensures traceable, non-mulesed wool and best practices are met from farm through production

The power of merino
17.5 micron merino is ultra-fine for super soft, comfortable feel, and is naturally thermoregulating, odor-resistant, and wicks sweat and moisture

Comfort from volcanic ash
Keeps you from getting too warm or too chilly — 37.5 Technologies® nylon with volcanic ash enhances thermoregulation and anti-stink properties

Put it on repeat
External back locker loop allows for hang drying and re-wearing to reduce water consumption

Multipurpose comfort
Layer under a long sleeve top on chilly days or on its own as it warms up
Materials + Specs

Repeat Merino
47% 17.5μ merino wool, 38% 37.5® nylon, 15% nylon

Made with natural fiber, this item may shrink slightly on first wash

Wonderful material, fit is slightly off and new logo reduces versatility

I really love Janji as a brand, so I don't love writing a critical review. The merino blend in this shirt is top notch. It is comfortable, smooth, and based on my prior experiences, holds up well.

I have two problems with this shirt. As you will see in other views, a common use case of this item is everyday wear. It can and should be a very versatile material. For a plain shirt, having a large rectangle logo on the front reduces that versatility and says "I'm a sport/running shirt".

Other than that, the fit of the shirt has notably changed from prior versions. The sleeves are longer. I find an odd fit under the arms. And the shirt is longer. Overall, the fit is large length-wise, but remains slim in the chest.
