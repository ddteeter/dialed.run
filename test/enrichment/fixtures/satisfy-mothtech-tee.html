    Usage

    From concrete to mud, rock to dust. Come heat, sweat, air. This is the top we reach for, everyday training, steady miles, and the silence that follows. Soft. Breathable.  It’s OK to run in cotton.

    Technology

    - MothTech™

    Engineered to mirror the wear patterns of shirts shaped by years of motion.

    Our ventilation system is body mapped and precision cut, openings placed where heat builds and where effort peaks. These “moth” perforations move air without breaking rhythm.

    Each tee balances softness with structure. Designed to perform. Built to endure. Overwashed organic cotton for comfort that begins on day one and only deepens over time.
