A merino-backed half tight that extends the freedom of the short into cool-to-cold conditions.

A lightweight woven fabric places a 24% merino wool inner face against the skin, adding warmth and helping regulate temperature as conditions and effort change. Enough protection to take the edge off colder days, without giving up the compact feel and freedom of a half-tight.

The waistband-free construction keeps the silhouette clean and the fit low-bulk. A vertical rear stuff pocket, adapted from our Speed Shorts, provides simple on-body storage, while reflective rear transfers increase visibility in low light.

Cold-weather running, cut above the knee.

Merino Inner Face

24% merino wool helps regulate temperature across cool-to-cold conditions.

Vertical Stuff Pocket

Rear storage keeps small essentials close and easy to reach on the move.

Waistband-Free Construction

Integrated construction removes the conventional waistband for a cleaner, lower-bulk fit.

Antibacterial

Wards off odours and keep the garment fresh run after run.
